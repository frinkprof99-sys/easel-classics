# Easel Classics

Public-domain paintings and prints, cropped to 16:9 at 3840×2160 for showing on a Samsung Frame TV. The Easel iPhone app downloads them from here on request.

Every image comes from a museum's open-access programme and is marked public domain (CC0) in that museum's own data:
- The Metropolitan Museum of Art (Open Access)
- Art Institute of Chicago
- Cleveland Museum of Art (Open Access)

`catalog.json` lists each work's title, artist, date, museum, licence and source page. The crops are Easel's. The originals are at the museums' pages.

The CC0 dedication means you may use these freely; credit to the museums is appreciated.
